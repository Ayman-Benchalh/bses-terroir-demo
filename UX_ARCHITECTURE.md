## 1. Homepage Rhythm & Composition

1. **§01 Editorial Cover:** Full-width nocturnal Aubergine band (`#352B3E`) with massive Fraunces italic headline, electric Citron CTA button, and cinematic edge-to-edge harvest photography.
2. **§02 Market Index (Category Discovery):** Responsive 2 / 3 / 6-column grid displaying the 6 agricultural universes with square-corner market tickets and clean typography.
3. **§03 Seasonal Selection (Primeurs du Moment):** 4 fresh produce items rendered in `.market-ticket` cards with geographic origin pins and quick-add stepper.
4. **§04 L'Arpenteur des Terroirs (Market Explorer):** Interactive geographic discovery tool showcasing 7 Moroccan regions (Berkane, Souss, Ouezzane, Taliouine, Tafilalet, Sefrou, Atlas) using SVG stamps and live product filtering.
5. **§05 Basket Builder Invitation:** Full-width Aubergine band offering custom weekly family crate assembly (*Panier sur Mesure*).
6. **§06 Terroir Specialties:** Spotlight on argan, raw honey, and pure Taliouine saffron.
7. **§07 The Living Terroir (Pillars & Heritage):** Mint-tinted editorial strip detailing the 3 commitments (Direct from Cooperatives, Zero Intermediaries, Fair Producer Compensation).
8. **§08 Delivery Simulation:** Interactive city simulator covering Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Fès, Tanger, and Agadir with instant fee and free-delivery threshold calculations.
